Prospective PhD Students

Bocconi University offers a four-year PhD program in Statistics and Computer Science, with coursework concentrated in the first year. Information about the program, admission requirements, and application deadlines can be found on the PhD in Statistics and Computer Science webpage. Applications typically close early in the calendar year. All PhD applications must go through Bocconi University's official admissions process. Please refer to the program's website for information about admission requirements and selection procedures. Contacting me directly is not necessary to apply.
