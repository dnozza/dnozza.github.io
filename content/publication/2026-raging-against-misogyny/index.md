abstract: 'Implicit misogyny often requires background knowledge to be understood.
  To address this challenge, we propose a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework
  for detecting and explaining implicit misogyny in English and Italian, leveraging
  encyclopedic, slang, and feminist knowledge sources. Using the ImplicIT-Mis and SBIC+
  datasets, we evaluate the contribution of each knowledge source to detection and
  explanation quality, investigating also whether RAG addresses known LLM failure modes
  in implicit misogyny. Results show that RAG improves recall on Italian data, but does
  not improve English detection performance. However, human evaluation shows that
  RAG-based explanations are preferred over no-RAG explanations in both languages.
  Feminist literature emerges as the most consistently useful source for detection
  and explanation.'
